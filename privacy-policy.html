Privacy Policy — Curious Aura

Last updated: June 2, 2026

"Curious Aura" ("the app") is a personal tool used by its owner to upload short educational videos to the owner's own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What we access

With your permission (the TikTok video.upload scope), the app uploads video files you created to your own TikTok account as drafts. It may read basic profile info (open id, display name) only to identify the connected account.

What we store

The app stores your TikTok access and refresh tokens locally on the owner's own computer so it can upload without logging in every time. These tokens are never shared with anyone or sent to any third party.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, store, or process other users' personal data.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Data deletion

You can revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok account settings, and delete the locally stored token file to remove all stored credentials.
